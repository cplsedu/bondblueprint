## THE BIG PICTURE: What This Audience Is Really About

These audiences are women (primarily) in one of three emotional states:
1. **Stuck** — in a situationship, confusing relationship, or post-breakup limbo
2. **Healing** — actively trying to become the next version of themselves
3. **Claiming** — in a manifestation/abundance mindset, affirming their future

Your content needs to speak to all three. The comments below reveal exactly what each group is saying.

### 🔥 PATTERN 1: Avoidant Behavior Obsession
**The single most commented-on topic across all accounts.**

People cannot stop talking about avoidants. They are endlessly confused, hurt, and fascinated.

**What they literally say:**
- *"Once you date an avoidant, you'll know the difference. They're super consistent until one day they flip a switch."*
- *"Avoidants need extensive therapy before they date anyone, simple as that."* (40 likes)
- *"I'm done with this question. I had an 18 year relationship with an avoidant."*
- *"The switch usually happens after a period of increased closeness, commitment, or conflict."*
- *"He came on strong then disappeared 🫠"*
- *"They said 'oh sure' and disappeared."*
- *"My ex was a FA and it pains me to see her dealing with so much pain."*
- *"This is exactly what happened to me yesterday with my DA partner."*
- *"As a FA, why did I only reflect after the breakup? Why didn't I see what I needed before?"*
- *"He deactivates — that's when it becomes clear."*
- *"I've been working really hard with my DA partner. What a difference it's making."*

**The core confusion:** They can't tell if someone is avoidant or just not interested. This generates massive debate and comments every single time.

**Content hook this creates for you:**
> *"Here's how to tell if he's avoidant — or just not into you (most people get this wrong)"*

### 🔥 PATTERN 2: "The Switch" — The Moment Everything Changed
**Emotionally the hottest micro-topic in this niche.**

People describe a specific moment when a relationship went from good to confusing — and they're obsessed with understanding it.

**What they literally say:**

**Why this works:** It validates a real desire (wanting to feel chosen) while offering a reframe (choosing yourself first). The debate in the comments IS the engagement.
