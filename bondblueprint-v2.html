🔒 Our Privacy Promise

We take your privacy seriously. Here's exactly what we do and don't do with your information.

What we store

Your name and email address (to identify your account and save your conversations).

Your conversation history, stored locally on your device and optionally on our server so you can access it across sessions.

Your message credit balance.

What we never do

We never sell your data to third parties. Ever.

We never share your conversation content with advertisers, marketers, or data brokers.

We never use your personal situation to train AI models without explicit consent.

We never display ads inside BondBlueprint.

How your conversations are used

Your situation description is sent to our AI service (Anthropic Claude) to generate a personalized response. Anthropic processes this under their privacy policy and does not use it to train models per their enterprise terms.

Conversations are stored encrypted and accessible only to you via your account.

Your rights

You can delete all your data at any time using the button below. This removes everything stored on this device immediately.
